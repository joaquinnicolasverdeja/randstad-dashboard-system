# Randstad Operational: Strategic HR Analysis and Computerized Dashboard System

**[Interactive dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/views/ComputarizedDashboardSystemforStrategicDecissionMaking-RandstadOperational-IndividualAssesment/Home)** · **[User guide](https://canva.link/8faiaofgrhud6ta)** · **[One-pager (PDF)](docs/one_pager.pdf)**

## Overview

The staffing industry is operating in a volatile, highly competitive landscape shaped by technological disruption, demographic shifts and changing client and talent expectations. This project assesses Randstad Operational's competitive position, diagnoses its key HR areas and proposes two strategic recommendations. The final deliverable is a computerized dashboard system in Tableau, with a user guide, to support managers' day-to-day strategic decisions.

MSc assessment case study based on Randstad Operational Business Unit. 

## Approach

| Level | Framework |
|---|---|
| Business environment | PESTEL |
| Industry | Porter's Five Forces, client-needs analysis |
| Competitive position vs. Adecco and Manpower | VRIO, Bowman's Strategy Clock |
| Key HR areas | Recruitment & Selection, Reward Management, Training & Development ("70-20-10" model) |

## Key findings

- **Differentiated position.** Randstad's value proposition is driven by proprietary in-house technology and talent development that starts at the recruitment stage.
- **Recruitment vulnerability.** The selection process is protected against right-to-work falsification, identity theft and false backgrounds, but not against unauthorised AI assistance during assessments.
- **Development gap.** The upskilling and reskilling offer meets current expectations, but talent increasingly demands personalised career pathways with genuine human interaction.

## Recommendations

**1. Shield the recruitment process against candidate cheating.** Integrate a remote proctoring solution into the Randstad Talent Platform to verify that a live human is taking the test, keep the candidate fully visible and lock the screen during assessments.
*KPIs:* Quality of Hire, Time to Performance, Involuntary Turnover Rate, Yield Rate, Fraud Detection Rate.

**2. Deliver a personalised, human-led career development approach.** Add personalised career pathways with human guidance to the existing learning offer, similar to Manpower's "My Path" strategy.
*KPIs:* Retention Rate, Voluntary Turnover Rate, Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS).

## The dashboard system

Built in Tableau, the system includes four dashboards covering the strategic analysis of key HR areas in line with staffing industry demands. It combines benchmarking metrics, Tableau's forecasting tool and "what-if" parameters so managers can explore scenarios. The [user guide](https://canva.link/8faiaofgrhud6ta) explains how to navigate each dashboard and documents the data sources.

## Data note

Where possible, the data reflects Randstad's public information. Data points that are confidential in a real company were simulated for this case study, and their sources are documented in the user guide. The dashboard is a demonstration of design and decision-support functionality: **its figures should not be read as real insights about Randstad.**
